## Summary

At 320 px an error toast raised in a dialog sits at the top of the screen over the dialog title, and every Save press adds another toast (up to three stacked) instead of replacing the last.

## Steps to reproduce

1. 320x640, English, Zones tab.
2. 'Advanced: add a zone manually'.
3. Press Save three times without filling anything in.

## Expected

One message at a time (a new one replaces the old), and the dialog title stays visible.

## Actual

Title at y 28-50; three toasts at y 12-52, 60-99, 107-147, the first covering the title.
