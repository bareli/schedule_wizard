## Summary

At 320 px an error toast raised in a dialog sits at the top of the screen over the dialog title, and every Save press adds another toast (up to three stacked) instead of replacing the last.

## Steps to reproduce

1. 320x640, English, Zones tab.
2. 'Advanced: add a zone manually'.
3. Press Save three times without filling anything in.

## Expected

One message at a time (a new one replaces the old), and the dialog title stays visible.

## Actual

Title at y 28-50; three toasts at y 12-52, 60-99, 107-147, the first covering the title.

## Evidence

![three stacked toasts over the Add zone title, 320 px](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/BUG-022-dialog-toasts-en-320-before.png?raw=1)

## Relevant files

custom_components/schedule_wizard/www/panel.js (`_toast`, `.toast-host`)

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

Fix commits `509425d`, `ed1b757` on `fix/v0.15.0-polish`. Full handback with recipe and environment: issue #83.

**Fix** (commits `509425d`, `ed1b757`, `www/panel.js`): `_toast` shows one message at a time (`host.replaceChildren(t)`, the newest replaces the last). An error raised in a dialog goes into the dialog right after its title (`h3`, or the wizard's `.wiz-head`), in the flow and `position: sticky; top: 0`, so it never covers the title and stays in view when the form is scrolled. Fields in dialogs get `scroll-margin-top: 84px`, so the field focused after Save is scrolled clear of the message with its caption visible. Messages outside dialogs are unchanged (bottom of the screen).
