## Summary

After Stop on a zone in the card, the rebuild puts keyboard focus on that zone's minutes field. The panel's rule (#41) is that Stop hands focus to the zone's Water now.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `/sw-card/0`, English, 1440.
2. Water now on Front lawn, wait for the poll.
3. Focus Stop (running block, or the zone list with `show_active: false`) and press Enter; wait 6.5 s.

## Expected

Focus on Front lawn's Water now.

## Actual

Both places: focus on `INPUT "Front lawn: minutes"`.

## Evidence

Evidence unavailable as a picture: focus position measured with the deep `document.activeElement` (quoted above).

## Relevant files

custom_components/schedule_wizard/www/card.js (`captureFocus` / `restoreFocus`)

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

Fix commits `509425d` on `fix/v0.15.0-polish`. Full handback with recipe and environment: issue #85.

**Fix** (commit `509425d`, `www/card.js`): `captureFocus` notes when the focused control is Stop (`button.stop`) or Water now (`button.run`); after the rebuild `restoreFocus` moves focus to the control that took its place in the same zone row (Stop -> Water now, Water now -> Stop) before any other fallback. This works whether Stop was in the running block or in the zone list.
