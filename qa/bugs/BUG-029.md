## Summary

The a11y verifier saw Reports 'Front lawn 19 runs / 98 min' while `get_state` `runs_7d` was 9. Investigation (8172 history): both count `completed` + `cancelled` rows in the last 7 x 24 h with the same rule; the two readings were taken at different times. Up to 14:31 Front lawn had 9 runs / 98 min; between 16:42 and 16:53 ten more runs were started and stopped within seconds (0 min each), giving 19 runs / 98 min. Not a counting bug. What is unclear is the column caption: '7d' / '30d' do not say the period in words.

## Steps to reproduce

Reports tab: per-zone table headers.

## Expected

Column captions name the period ('Last 7 days', 'Last 30 days') in every language.

## Actual

'7d' / '30d' (de '7 T.').

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

Fix commits `509425d` on `fix/v0.15.0-polish`. Full handback with recipe and environment: issue #90.

**Finding** (the check the brief asked for): not a counting bug. Reports and `get_state` count the same rows (`completed` + `cancelled`, last 7 x 24 h). The 8172 history shows Front lawn with 9 runs / 98 min up to 14:31; between 16:42 and 16:53 ten more runs were started and stopped within seconds (0 min each), so the verifier's two readings (9, then 19 runs / 98 min) were taken at different times.

**Fix** (commit `509425d`, `www/i18n.js`): the per-zone Reports columns say the period: "Last 7 days" / "Last 30 days" (de "Letzte 7 Tage" / "Letzte 30 Tage", he "7 הימים האחרונים" / "30 הימים האחרונים", all 17 languages) instead of "7d" / "30d".
