## Summary

A plan created today at 18:24 shows a greyed past run "Today 6:03 AM" in This week (and the calendar entity has an event for it), although the plan did not exist then and nothing ran.

## Steps to reproduce

Create a plan every 2 days from today, 30 min before sunrise, in the evening. Open Home > This week.

## Expected

Occurrences before the schedule was created are not shown (not as past runs, not in the calendar).

## Actual

Today column: "6:03 AM Morning watering · 30 min", greyed as past.

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01): BUG-032

**Fix** (`9c03ee5`, `planner.occurrences`): a run whose start is before the schedule's `created_at` is not an occurrence. This covers This week (`get_state.week`), the watering calendar entity (events and state) and the card. Schedules without `created_at` behave as before.
