## Summary

German voice reply for one minute: "Bewässere Rasen für 1 Minuten." (also "für 1 Tage pausiert", and English "for 1 minutes"). Rain pause notification for 1.5 h: "Bewässerung für 1.5 Std. pausiert (Regen)": `fmt_number` always writes a decimal point.

## Steps to reproduce

`REPLIES['de']['started'].format(minutes=1)`; `notify_text('de', 'rain_pause_all', hours=fmt_number(1.5))`.

## Expected

"für 1 Minute", "1 Tag"; numbers in notifications with the language's decimal separator ("1,5 Std.") in all 17 languages.

## Actual

"Bewässere Rasen für 1 Minuten.", "Bewässerung für 1 Tage pausiert.", "Bewässerung für 1.5 Std. pausiert (Regen)".

## Evidence

Not an interface defect; strings quoted above.

## Relevant files

custom_components/schedule_wizard/voice.py, notify_text.py, scheduler.py, __init__.py
