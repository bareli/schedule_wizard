## Summary

The check line under the rain source reads entity states captured when Settings was drawn. Re-selecting a sensor, or a state change while Settings is open, still shows the old "Now:".

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Settings, pick a rain sensor (line shows "Now: off").
2. Change that entity's state, or pick another sensor and back.

## Expected

The line shows the current state, without re-rendering the form or stealing focus.

## Actual

Old state stays until Settings is redrawn. No screenshot: no browser available for this pass (Evidence unavailable).

## Likely root cause

`_rainPicker` captures `const states = this._hass.states` once; `update()` reads that snapshot.

## Relevant files

`custom_components/schedule_wizard/www/panel.js` (`_rainPicker`, `set hass`).
