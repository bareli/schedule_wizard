## Summary

History rows with status `cancelled` and no `planned_min` were written by v0.14.x with the planned length in `duration_min`, not the watered minutes. The panel's "Last watering" line, day totals, Reports and the server's `runs_7d` / `total_min_7d` count them at full planned length. Legacy `superseded` rows are already excluded.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Keep a v0.14.x history with a stopped run (`cancelled`, planned 10 min, stopped after 2).
2. Open Activity and Reports on 0.15.0.

## Expected

Minutes of such a row are left out of watered totals (same rule as legacy `superseded`); the run still counts as a run where cancelled runs counted before. Server and panel agree.

## Actual

Seen on dev: total 130 min when only 2 min watered today.
