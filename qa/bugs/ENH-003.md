## Problem

The brief task is "every other day at dawn". The product takes only HH:MM. Sunrise moves by several hours across the year at German latitudes (about 1.5 hours or more at Israeli ones), so a fixed 06:00 is dawn in one month and daylight or dark in another. Gardeners say "before sunrise", not "06:00". This is not a defect; no requirement asks for it.

## Why it matters

Dawn is the standard advice for watering; users otherwise edit times by hand twice a year or water in the dark or the midday heat.

## Evidence

![Wizard step 3 and the editor have a single clock-time field](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-009-wizard-when-step-weekdays-only.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Add "At: clock time / Sunrise / Sunset" with an offset in minutes (before / after), computed from the HA location and time zone. Show the resulting time in the existing "First run" line. Needs a product decision on offsets and edge cases (model:opus).

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01): ENH-003 sunrise / sunset start

**What changed**
- Schedules have `time_mode` = `clock` (default, every existing schedule) | `sunrise` | `sunset` and `sun_offset_min` (-180..180, negative = before). Stored schedules are not rewritten; a schedule without `time_mode` is a clock schedule.
- `planner.fire_at(sched, local_date, hass)` gives the start of each local date: HA's `get_astral_event_date` at the HA location (same as the `sun` entity), picking the event whose **local** date is that day (Astral works on UTC dates; checked for Auckland and Los Angeles), offset added in real minutes, then to the minute. The cron loop, next run (zone cards, `sensor.schedule_wizard_next_schedule`), week view, `calendar.schedule_wizard_watering_schedule`, reminders and Skip next all use it.
- Edge cases (decided here, documented in README): an offset that would cross midnight runs on its own day at 00:00 or 23:59; a day without sunrise/sunset (polar) has no run.
- Services `add_schedule` / `update_schedule`: `time_mode`, `sun_offset_minutes`; `time` is optional with sunrise/sunset (the clock time is kept so switching back restores it); on update a `time` without `time_mode` means a clock time; `sun_offset_minutes` with a clock time is an error. New WS `schedule_wizard/preview_schedule` (next start times of a schedule being edited).
- Panel schedule editor only (wizard step 3 unchanged, per the brief to keep panel edits to the new fields): **Start at** (A set time / Sunrise / Sunset); for the sun, **Minutes before or after** [0-180] [before/after], a hint, and "Next runs: ..." from the server. Inline error for a bad offset (server error mapped to the same field). Lists show "Every day, 30 min before sunrise". Changing a zone keeps the sun rule of its watering times. 17 languages.
