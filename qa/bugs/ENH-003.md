## Problem

The brief task is "every other day at dawn". The product takes only HH:MM. Sunrise moves by several hours across the year at German latitudes (about 1.5 hours or more at Israeli ones), so a fixed 06:00 is dawn in one month and daylight or dark in another. Gardeners say "before sunrise", not "06:00". This is not a defect; no requirement asks for it.

## Why it matters

Dawn is the standard advice for watering; users otherwise edit times by hand twice a year or water in the dark or the midday heat.

## Evidence

![Wizard step 3 and the editor have a single clock-time field](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-009-wizard-when-step-weekdays-only.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Add "At: clock time / Sunrise / Sunset" with an offset in minutes (before / after), computed from the HA location and time zone. Show the resulting time in the existing "First run" line. Needs a product decision on offsets and edge cases (model:opus).
