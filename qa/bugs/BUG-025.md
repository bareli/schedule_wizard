## Summary

The Settings 'More options' `<details>` is exposed as a group with no name, and so are the 13 option groups inside it (`details.opt-group`) once it is open.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Settings tab, English.
2. CDP `Accessibility.getFullAXTree`, list role=group nodes; open More options and list again.

## Expected

Each group is named by its visible caption (More options, and each option group's title).

## Actual

`group "" details.more` and 13 x `group "" details.opt-group`; the other groups on the page are named ("Send notifications to", "Send notification when").

## Evidence

Evidence unavailable as a picture: accessibility-tree output quoted above (nothing visible changes).

## Relevant files

custom_components/schedule_wizard/www/panel.js (`_renderSettings`, `_optGroup`)

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

Fix commits `509425d` on `fix/v0.15.0-polish`. Full handback with recipe and environment: issue #86.

**Fix** (commit `509425d`, `www/panel.js`): `details.more` gets `aria-labelledby` its `summary` ("More options"); each `details.opt-group` gets `aria-labelledby` the `strong` title inside its summary (the on/off pill and the description are not part of the name).
