## Summary

When a run is stopped early, the history row keeps the planned `duration_min` and status `cancelled`. Reports (Per-zone totals, Last 30 days bar) and the Zones card line ("1 runs / 10 min last 7 days") add that planned figure. A gardener who stops the sprinkler after two minutes reads "10 min". The data the reports exist to show (how much water, how long) is overstated, and the error is invisible. Product call: record actual elapsed minutes on cancel (hence model:opus).

## Steps to reproduce

1. Home, Water now on Front lawn (10 min). After ~3 s press Stop watering and confirm. Repeat 4 times.
2. Home > Reports.

## Expected

Per-zone totals about 4 runs and well under 1 min each (actual open time). Source: Reports is described as totals of watering; inference, not a written rule.

## Actual

Front lawn "4x / 40 min" for 7d, 30d and Total; the Last 30 days chart shows a full bar for today. History rows from get_state: `status: cancelled, duration_min: 10, note: manual stop`, started at ts 1790849026, cancelled at 1790849029 (3 s). panel.js (~2475, `status === "completed" || status === "cancelled"` adds `dur`).

## Evidence

![Per-zone totals: Front lawn 4x / 40 min after four 3-second runs](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/BUG-013-reports-planned-minutes.png?raw=1)

Also in the same screenshot: the "Skip reasons" tile "Cancelled 5" (UX-003 already noted Cancelled is not a skip reason) and "Based on the last 10 history entries (cap 500)".

## Recommendation

On cancel / stop, write the elapsed minutes (rounded, at least 1 if the valve opened) into the history row, keep the planned value in a separate field, and let Reports sum actual. Add a regression test for stop-after-N-seconds.
