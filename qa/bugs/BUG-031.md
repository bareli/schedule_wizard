## Summary

The "First run" line under Every N days prints the raw clock value (`06:00`) while the rest of the page formats times in the user's locale (`6:05 AM` in English).

## Steps to reproduce

Home > + New watering plan > pick zones > Next > Every 2 to 30 days, A set time 06:00 (UI en).

## Expected

"Today's 6:00 AM has passed. First run: Sat, Oct 3 6:00 AM, then every 2 days" (same formatter as the rest of the page; 24 h where the locale uses it).

## Actual

"Today's 06:00 has passed. First run: Sat, Oct 3 06:00, then every 2 days".
