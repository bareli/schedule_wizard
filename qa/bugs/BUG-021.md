## Summary

In Hebrew at 320 px every tab scrolls sideways by 1 px. The persistent live region uses `.sr-only { position: absolute; margin: -1px; }` with no inset, so in RTL its static position sits at the right edge and pokes 1 px past the viewport.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Panel in Hebrew, 320x800.
2. Any tab.
3. `scrollTo(1000, 0)`; read `scrollX`, and `getBoundingClientRect()` of `.sr-only`.

## Expected

No horizontal scroll in any language.

## Actual

he 320: `.sr-only` at left 320 / right 321, `document.scrollingElement.scrollWidth` 321 vs `clientWidth` 320, `scrollX` reaches 1. en 320: left -1 / right 0, no scroll. he 1440: no scroll (sidebar shown).

## Evidence

Evidence unavailable as a picture: 1 px of scroll does not show in a screenshot; the measurement above is the evidence.

## Relevant files

custom_components/schedule_wizard/www/panel.js (`.sr-only`)

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

Fix commits `509425d` on `fix/v0.15.0-polish`. Full handback with recipe and environment: issue #82.

**Fix** (commit `509425d`, `www/panel.js`): `.sr-only` is pinned with `top: 0; left: 0; margin: 0` (plus `clip-path: inset(50%)`), so its position no longer depends on the text direction and nothing pokes past the right edge in RTL.
