## Problem

Measured bounding boxes (CSS px): `.btn.small` "Skip day" 97x20, other small buttons 74x24 (Zones), switches 40x22, stepper buttons 32x34, tab 48x39, link button 133x23. WCAG 2.1 AA has no target-size criterion (2.5.5, 44x44, is AAA). WCAG 2.2 adds 2.5.8 at AA with a 24x24 minimum and a spacing exception; the 20 px and 22 px items would fall short of it. I did not re-verify the current text of 2.5.8 (`Unverified`; an authoritative copy of the WCAG 2.2 recommendation would settle it). The product declares no standard, so this is an enhancement, not a conformance failure.

## Why it matters

Skip day sits next to the day title on a phone, and the 22 px switch toggles a schedule that waters a garden; a missed tap on a 20 px target is likely.

## Evidence

![small Skip day links in the week row, switches in the Zones rows](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/BUG-006-primary-colour-contrast-light.png?raw=1)

The same Home screenshot as BUG-006 ("Skip day" links in the week). Sizes from `getBoundingClientRect()` on the live panel, 2026-10-01; card buttons were not measured separately (CSS `padding: 4px 10px; font-size: 12px`, so roughly 26 px high).

## Recommendation

Raise `.btn.small`, `.switch`, `.wk-head .btn` and the card buttons to at least 24 px high (44 px on touch via `@media (pointer: coarse)`).
