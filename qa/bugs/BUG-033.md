## Summary

In a one-at-a-time plan (Front lawn 10, Herbs 10, back 10 min) every zone card says "Next: Sat 6:05 AM": the plan's start. Herbs really starts at 6:15 and the back garden at 6:25.

## Steps to reproduce

Plan with three zones, 10 min each, one at a time. Home > Your zones.

## Expected

Each zone shows its own estimated start, in the scheduler's real order (sequential, or interleaved cycle & soak).

## Actual

All three: "Next: Sat 6:05 AM · 10 min"; card: "Next: Sat 6:05 AM (in 1d)" for each zone.

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01): BUG-033

**Choice:** each zone's own start. The scheduler's order is deterministic, so it is computable: new `Scheduler.zone_start_offsets(cycle_id)` replays `_cycle_plan` with `_run_steps_sequential` (sum of the earlier steps incl. their soak pauses) or `_run_steps_interleaved` (the next zone waters during a soak), with today's temperature factor. What it cannot know in advance: a zone skipped at run time (rain, moisture) moves later zones earlier, and the temperature factor can change by then.

**Fix** (`9c03ee5`, `scheduler.py`, `__init__.py` `_next_run_for`): a zone's `next_run.fires_at` / `in_seconds` / `time_label` are its own start; `next_run.plan_starts_at` keeps the plan's start; a skipped run (`next_skip.fires_at`) moves the same way. Zone cards and the card read these, so both change.
