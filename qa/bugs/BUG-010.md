Declared standard: none in `qa/PROJECT.md`, WCAG 2.1 AA assumed. Tooling: Chromium 153 + Playwright 1.63.0.

## Summary

At a 320 px viewport the German Reports view is 381 px wide: the per-plan table's headers ("Übersprungen", "Gesamt") are wider than their grid columns and push the page into a horizontal scrollbar. English and Hebrew at 320 px, and every other view in en / he / de at 320 px and 640 px (200 % zoom of 1280), do not overflow.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Set the user language to German, viewport 320x700, Home, Reports (at least one plan registered).
2. Compare `documentElement.scrollWidth` with 320; list elements whose right edge exceeds 321.

## Expected

No horizontal scrolling at 320 CSS px for content other than data that needs two dimensions (1.4.10). The per-plan table is small enough to reflow, so it does not meet the exception.

## Actual

`scrollWidth` 381; offenders `SPAN.num` (right edges 333 and 381): the "Übersprungen" and "Gesamt" header cells of the plan table (grid `1.5fr repeat(3, 0.7fr) 0.7fr`).

## Evidence

![German reports at 320 px, plan table header clipped](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/BUG-010-reports-de-320.png?raw=1)

320x700, light theme, German. "Übersprung..." is cut at the card edge and the page scrolls sideways. Code: `panel.js` L2600-2619.

## Likely root cause

Fixed fractional grid columns for the per-plan table; long German header words cannot shrink. Likely.

## Relevant files

`custom_components/schedule_wizard/www/panel.js` (`_renderReports`).

## Recommended remediation

`minmax(0, ...)` columns with `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on headers, or stack the table below 400 px. Check all 17 languages at 320 px.

## Regression test

A11Y-09: browser check of `scrollWidth <= 320` on Reports in de, ru, he, en. A pytest test cannot measure layout.
