## Problem

The wizard's first step lists `switch.schedule_wizard_rain_delay` next to the real zones. After zones exist, the Edit zone list also offers `switch.front_lawn_watering`, `switch.herbs_watering`, `switch.morning_watering_enabled` and `switch.gynh_khvryt_watering`, which are the integration's own entities (each zone and plan creates one). Picking one points a zone at itself or at the plan's enable switch; in the wizard, a gardener ticking "Schedule Wizard rain delay" would make a rain delay a sprinkler. The list is long (7 candidates for 3 zones on a nearly empty HA; hundreds on a real one) and shows input_boolean first.

## Why it matters

The cost of a wrong tap is a loop or a "zone" that never waters, with no hint why.

## Evidence

![Edit zone list shows switch.*_watering and the rain-delay switch](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-015-picker-own-switches.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Exclude entities created by this integration from every picker (entity registry platform = schedule_wizard). Optional: sort registered/likely domains (valve, switch) first. Strings unchanged, so sonnet can fix.

## Kind

Works, could be better (prevents an easy mistake).

## Prototype

Not needed.
