## Problem

With persistent_notification selected for zone started / finished and paused-for-rain, the texts were: "Opened Front lawn for 10 min (service)", "Closed Front lawn (cancelled)", "Rain delay set for 24.0h", "Rain delay cleared". HA language of qa_admin is Hebrew (hass.language = he); the texts are English. UX-002 translated only the temperature skip; the developer's own note on #31 left rain, moisture, forecast and condition notifications English, and these start/finish/rain-delay texts too (scheduler.py f-strings, __init__.py:801).

Wording: "Opened/Closed" is valve talk for a sprinkler; "(service)" and "(cancelled)" are internal states; "24.0h" prints a float; "Rain delay cleared" does not say which zones; every title is "Schedule Wizard" so a phone shows ten identical headers.

## Why it matters

German and Hebrew users get the push on their lock screen in English with developer vocabulary. It is the most-read surface of the product after the panel.

## Evidence

![HA notifications drawer: English, 24.0h, no zones named](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-013-notifications-english-24-0h.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Run all notification bodies through the same 17-language table the reminders use. Proposed en: "Front lawn started, 10 min" / "Front lawn finished" / "Front lawn stopped" / "Watering paused for 24 h (rain)". Title = the zone or plan name. Owner to approve wording (model:opus).

## Kind

Works, could be better.

Not needed (copy only).
