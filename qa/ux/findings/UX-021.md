## Problem

In Hebrew the new number + unit strings are reordered by the bidi algorithm, so they read backwards or scrambled:

- Week list day header, en "30 °C / 18 °C · ≈ 302 L", he renders as **"C / 18 °C · ≈ 302° 30 ליטר"**; Monday as "C / 14 °C · 2.0° 24 מ״מ גשם · ≈ 302 ליטר". The max temperature and the litres swap places with the min.
- Frost tip title "סכנת קרה מחר: עד 1 °C" renders as **"עד C° 1"**.
- Weather tips settings: labels "יום חם מ־(°C)" show as "(C°)", the "Extra water on hot days" select shows **"% 30+"** instead of "+30 %".
- Same pattern in the boost banner ("השקיה +30 % ...").

## Why it matters

The owner tests Hebrew first. The forecast numbers are the reason the tip exists; a gardener who reads "302° 30" cannot tell the day's heat or water use, and a frost tip that shows "C° 1" looks broken.

## Recommendation

Isolate every value + unit as an LTR run (panel.js already has `ltr()`, line 515, used for the boost chooser buttons): `_fmtTemp`, `_fmtLiters`, the "{mm} mm" / "{pct} %" substitutions in `tips.*`, `week.water_est`, `week.rain_mm`, `boost.*` and the `set.tips_*` option labels. In the day header render each fact (temperature range, rain, litres) as its own span (or join with an RLM-safe separator) instead of one bidi-mixed string. Add a he check to the i18n test or a Playwright snapshot of one day header.
