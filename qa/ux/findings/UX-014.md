## Problem

1. One plan card holds two control groups that look alike: the time row (Undo, an on/off switch, **Edit**, **Delete**) and the plan row (**Run now**, **Edit**, **Disable**, **Delete**). Which Delete removes the plan and which the time? Which turns watering off, the switch or Disable? Both labels sit about 200 px apart.
2. Naming: the tab says **Programs**, the page title **Watering plans**, the Home button "+ New watering plan", the editor "Watering plan", and the card's "3 zones, 30 min total". The tab never matches the page it opens.
3. The time editor's "Enabled" is a bare checkbox while the list uses a switch for the same property.

## Why it matters

Two Delete buttons on one card is the classic wrong-tap on a destructive control; the gardener first has to decide which one means what.

## Evidence

![Programs: Edit and Delete twice, switch and Disable](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-014-programs-duplicate-controls.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Group the time row visibly (indented, "Watering times" heading, one overflow menu or icon buttons) and keep plan actions in the card header; drop Disable or the switch; rename the tab "Plans" (he/de to match) or the title "Programs". Owner picks the term (model:opus).
