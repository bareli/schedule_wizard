## Problem

1. Wizard counts steps by a rule the user cannot see: step 1 reads "Step 1 of 3" (3 progress segments); after ticking zones it becomes "Step 2 of 5" (5 segments). The bar visibly rebuilds.
2. Zones: "Last: 1m ago . 10 min . cancelled . **1 runs** / 10 min last 7 days"; "Never run . no runs last 7 days" says the same thing twice; each row prints the raw entity id (`input_boolean.zone_front`) as the second line, jargon on the screen gardeners use most.
3. Reports: "Based on the last 10 history entries (**cap 500**). Older entries are dropped." exposes an implementation limit; the chart has no minute scale, only the first, middle and last date; the per-zone table packs "4x / 40 min" in one cell.
4. Settings: "Allow plans at the same time: **Off** keeps one plan at a time so water pressure stays good." The word Off is both the state and the explanation (double negative). The notification checkbox "Skipped: temperature adjustment 0 % (too cool)" is long next to "Skipped (rain)".
5. **Stop watering** (the emergency button) and **Skip day** use the browser's native confirm() ("Stop all watering? This stops every running zone, plan and soak pause."). Stopping water is a one-tap job when a hose bursts; the native dialog is unstyled and gives no way to undo afterwards.

## Why it matters

Each is cheap; together they are the difference between "polished" and "a developer's tool".

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01): UX-018

Owner ruling: implement the record's recommendation.

**Fix** (`a50e935`, `www/panel.js`, `www/i18n.js`), per point:
1. Wizard: always 4 steps (Choose zones, When, Order / Name, Check); a new zone is named right under its tick box on step 1. "Step 1 of 4" stays "of 4".
2. Zones: no raw entity id line (it is in the tooltip and in Edit zone); "Never run" alone; "1 run" (singular was already fixed).
3. Reports: "Based on the last {n} watering records." (the 500 limit is only said once reached: "Based on the latest 500 records. Older ones are not kept."); a minute scale label above the bars; per-zone cells "4 runs" and "40 min" on two lines.
4. Settings: "Normally one plan runs at a time, so water pressure stays good."; the notification checkbox reads "Skipped (too cool)" (the notification text itself keeps the #31 wording).
5. Stop watering, Skip day, every Delete and Rotate webhook URL ask in an in-page dialog (`_confirm()` on the `_openDialog` helper: role alertdialog, panel language and direction, Escape / outside click = Cancel). Stop watering opens with the focus on its red button (one more tap or Enter in an emergency); deletes open on Cancel.
