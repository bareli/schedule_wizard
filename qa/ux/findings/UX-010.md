## Problem

After "Skip day" on Friday, the status card correctly says "Next: Morning watering, Mon 6:00 AM", but each zone card still says "Next: Fri 6:00 AM . 10 min" (he, 320: "הבא: יום ו׳ 6:00" under a status of "יום ב׳"). During a 24 h rain pause the zone cards show a "Paused" pill and still "Next: Fri 6:00 AM". Programs correctly shows "Next run skipped" with Undo. Three places answer "when does this zone water next" and two disagree. ENH-001 (#29) covered the 0 % warning only.

## Why it matters

The gardener who just skipped Friday because it rained looks at the zone, sees Friday 6:00, and either doubts the skip or presses it again (toggling it back). Trust in the single most important control ("skip") drops.

## Evidence

![Status says Mon 6:00, every zone card says Fri 6:00 AM](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-010-zone-next-vs-status-next.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Zone cards use the same next-run source as the status card: next run that will actually fire, or "Skipped Fri, next Mon 6:00 AM" / "Paused until Fri 1:09 PM". Strings can be built from existing keys; sonnet can implement once the owner picks between "show next real run" and "show next run, flagged skipped".

## Kind

Current behaviour is misleading (information contradicts itself); not a requirement, so filed as UX.

Offered in the report.

**Fix** (`a50e935`, `planner.py` `next_zone_run`, `__init__.py` get_state, `www/panel.js`): one source for "when does this zone water next".
- get_state gives each zone `next_run` = the next run that will water it (a day skipped by the user and a rain pause covering the zone, its own or the global one unless the zone is indoor, are passed over) and `next_skip` = `{fires_at, reason: skipped_manual | rain_delay, until}` for the run it drops.
- Zone cards (owner's two options combined): "Skipped Fri 6:00 AM. Next: Mon 6:00 AM · 10 min", during a rain pause "Paused until Sat 1:00 PM. Next: Mon 6:00 AM · 10 min", "... Nothing else planned" if no run follows. The status card's "Next" also passes over a fully paused run. The Lovelace card uses the same fields (#71).
