## Problem

1. "Skip watering when it rains, using" is a free-text box with the placeholder "weather.home or sensor.rain_forecast". The zone pickers elsewhere are searchable lists; this one must be typed from memory.
2. Typing `banana` and pressing Save settings shows "Saved at 1:01:34 PM." in the panel with no warning. The product does detect it (a Repair "missing_rain_entity_banana" appeared and raised the HA Settings badge from 2 to 3) but only in HA's Repairs screen, not where the user typed it. Clearing the box removed the repair.
3. Once an entity is chosen, what counts as rain lives in More options > Rain details (comma-separated states "rainy,pouring,snowy,lightning-rainy", attribute, numeric threshold), a second place for one decision.
4. "Send notifications to" lists raw service names: `persistent_notification` and `send_message`. A gardener cannot tell what "send_message" reaches; mobile_app targets would read `mobile_app_pixel_8`.

## Why it matters

"Skip when it rained" is one of the three jobs a gardener came for. A typo is accepted silently and the garden gets watered in the rain while the panel said Saved.

## Evidence

![Rain source 'banana' accepted: Saved at 1:01:34 PM, no warning](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-011-rain-entity-free-text-banana-saved.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Entity picker limited to weather.* and sensor.* with a "none" option; inline warning under the field when the entity does not exist (reuse the repair text); show the default rain states as a one-line sentence under the picker; friendly names for notify targets ("Phone: Pixel 8", "HA notifications panel"). Wording and picker design are product calls (model:opus).

## Kind

Works, could be better (point 2 is a silent-failure risk, so the strongest of the four).
