## Problem

The first-run wizard (5 steps) asks for weekday buttons, a start time and minutes per zone. There is no Repeat choice, so "every other day", the feature 0.14.0 was built for and the one the forum asked for, is only reachable after finishing the wizard: Programs, Edit on the time, Repeat, Every 2 to 30 days. The wizard also never mentions rain: the "skip when it rained" setting sits in Settings > Essentials, and nothing on Home or in the wizard's last step points there. On dev the conversion took Programs, Edit on the time, Repeat select, then the interval fields: four extra steps in a different tab.

## Why it matters

A new gardener who wants "every other day" picks Mon/Wed/Fri, which waters 3 days one week and 4 the next, never every other day, and has no signal that a better option exists. The wizard is the only guided path; features outside it are found by people who already know them.

![Wizard step 3: weekday buttons only](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-009-wizard-when-step-weekdays-only.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Add the same Repeat choice to step 3 (Days of the week / Every N days with first-watering date and the "First run: ..." line already built). Offer a last-step line "Skip when it rains? Choose a weather source" linking to Settings, or a Home nudge until a rain source is set. Wording needs the owner (model:opus).

Works, could be better (a feature gap in the guided flow).
