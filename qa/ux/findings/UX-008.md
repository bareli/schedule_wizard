## Problem

`section.status` carries `aria-live="polite"`, but the section itself is destroyed and re-created by every render (MutationObserver: 3 child replacements of `#app` in 11 s; `section.status` node identity changed). A live region that is replaced rather than updated is not reliably announced; where it is, the whole card (title, countdown, buttons' parent text) would be re-read on every refresh. A zone starting, stopping or finishing, and a rain pause starting, therefore have no dependable announcement. The only other feedback is a "Done" toast (`role=status`, 2.8 s, visually broken per BUG-012).

## Why it matters

A user who starts a valve with a screen reader gets no reliable confirmation that water is running or has stopped. **Whether any given screen reader announces this was not tested: no real screen reader was available, so the announcement behaviour is INCONCLUSIVE.** The DOM facts above are measured.

## Evidence

Evidence unavailable as an image: announcements and node identity are not visible. Measured 2026-10-01: after starting Front lawn from Home, `window.__first !== current section.status` after 11 s, 3 mutations on `#app`; toast `role=status` text "Done". Code: `panel.js` L995 (`aria-live` on a per-render node), L706-711 (`_toast`).

## Recommendation

Keep one persistent polite live region (visually hidden) outside the re-rendered area and write short messages into it on state changes ("Front lawn watering started, 10 minutes", "Watering stopped", "Paused for rain until Fri 1:07 PM"); do not announce the countdown. Verify with NVDA and VoiceOver.

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

**Fix** (commit `3ae4dc7`, `www/panel.js`): one persistent, visually hidden `role="status" aria-live="polite"` region created once in `_init`, outside `#app` (never re-rendered). `_refresh` compares the previous and the new state and writes only: "<zone>: watering started, <n> min", "<zone>: watering stopped", "No outdoor watering until <when>" (rain pause on or extended) and "Rain pause ended". Nothing on the first load and nothing on a countdown tick. `section.status` is no longer `aria-live`. New keys `live.started`, `live.stopped`, `live.rain_cleared` in 17 languages.
