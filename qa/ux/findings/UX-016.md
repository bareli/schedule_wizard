## Problem

1. While Front lawn runs, the card shows it twice: in the active block (name, "9:59 remaining . service", Stop, progress bar) and again in the list (name, dot, "9:59 remaining", Run greyed, Stop, progress bar). Two Stop buttons and two bars for one valve; at 320 px it takes a third of the screen.
2. The card registers with `window.customCards` (name "Schedule Wizard", description in English) but has no visual editor and no stub config: title, `valves`, `show_active`, `show_quick_run` are YAML only. The picker preview is off.
3. Vocabulary differs from the panel: the card says **Run / Stop / idle** (he "במנוחה"), the panel **Water now / Stop watering / Off**; the minutes box is a bare number with no "min" unit (the unit appears only in the small "default 10 min" text).
4. The card shows "Next: Fri 6:00 AM (in 17h)" with the same skipped-day / paused blind spot as UX-010, and has no pause-for-rain or skip control.

## Why it matters

The card is what many users put on their phone home screen; the panel is the second place they go. Duplicated rows and different words make the card look like a different product.

## Evidence

![Card at 320: Front lawn appears twice, two Stop buttons](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-016-card-running-zone-twice-320.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Hide a zone from the list while it appears in the active block (or merge them); add getConfigElement / getStubConfig with an entity multi-picker; align the verbs (Water now / Stop) and add "min" next to the field. Product decisions (model:opus). Unlabelled-input accessibility is covered by the A11Y record.
