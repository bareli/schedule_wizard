## Problem

1. While Front lawn runs, the card shows it twice: in the active block (name, "9:59 remaining . service", Stop, progress bar) and again in the list (name, dot, "9:59 remaining", Run greyed, Stop, progress bar). Two Stop buttons and two bars for one valve; at 320 px it takes a third of the screen.
2. The card registers with `window.customCards` (name "Schedule Wizard", description in English) but has no visual editor and no stub config: title, `valves`, `show_active`, `show_quick_run` are YAML only. The picker preview is off.
3. Vocabulary differs from the panel: the card says **Run / Stop / idle** (he "במנוחה"), the panel **Water now / Stop watering / Off**; the minutes box is a bare number with no "min" unit (the unit appears only in the small "default 10 min" text).
4. The card shows "Next: Fri 6:00 AM (in 17h)" with the same skipped-day / paused blind spot as UX-010, and has no pause-for-rain or skip control.

## Why it matters

The card is what many users put on their phone home screen; the panel is the second place they go. Duplicated rows and different words make the card look like a different product.

## Evidence

![Card at 320: Front lawn appears twice, two Stop buttons](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-016-card-running-zone-twice-320.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

Hide a zone from the list while it appears in the active block (or merge them); add getConfigElement / getStubConfig with an entity multi-picker; align the verbs (Water now / Stop) and add "min" next to the field. Product decisions (model:opus). Unlabelled-input accessibility is covered by the A11Y record.

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

Owner ruling: implement the record's recommendation. Done for points 1 to 3; point 4 is not part of the recommendation (see below).

**Fix** (commit `1ebce04`, `www/card.js`, `www/i18n.js`, README card section):
1. **Running zone once.** New exported `cardSections(state, config)` decides the two sections: a zone shown in the running block (running, or in a standalone soak pause) is left out of the list below. With `show_active: false` there is no running block, so every zone stays in the list. A list row now has one button: **Water now**, or **Stop watering** while that zone runs or soaks (the always-present Stop, which did nothing on an idle zone, is gone). Narrower rows at 320 px as a result.
2. **Visual editor.** `getConfigElement()` returns `schedule-wizard-card-editor` (ha-form: Title text, "Zones to show (empty shows all)" entity multi-picker limited to Schedule Wizard zones via `include_entities` from `get_state`, two toggles); `getStubConfig()` = `{title: "Schedule Wizard", show_active: true, show_quick_run: true}`; `customCards` preview on. Clearing the picker removes `valves` (an empty list also means all zones now; it used to show none). Labels in all 17 languages.
3. **Panel words.** Water now / Stop watering / Off (pill) from the panel's own keys (`zone.water_now`, `home.stop_watering`, `zone.off`); "min" next to every minutes field (`unit.min` template, so he reads "10 דק׳").
4. **Not done:** next-run line ignoring skipped days / pause, and a pause-for-rain control on the card. Not in the recommendation; the next-run blind spot is UX-010's subject. Say if you want it filed separately.

New keys (all 17 languages): `card.editor_title`, `card.editor_zones`, `card.editor_show_active`, `card.editor_show_quick_run` (plus the two naming keys of #47).
