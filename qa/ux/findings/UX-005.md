## Problem

The schedule dialog shows errors the right way: text under the field, `role="alert"`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, focus moved to the field (tested: interval blank gives the message and focus on the field). The zone and plan dialogs do not. A missing entity, zone name, plan name or step only produces a toast: no `aria-invalid` on any field (0 found), no required marking (0 `required` / `aria-required`), focus stays on Save. The toast is `role="status"` (polite, meant for confirmations, not errors) and removes itself after 2.8 s. Related and overlapping with BUG-012 (the toast is also unstyled and below the viewport, so sighted users do not see it at all); this record is about what remains after that is fixed.

## Why it matters

A person who reads slowly, magnifies, or uses a screen reader may miss a 2.8 s message and cannot tell which field it concerned. Not a clear WCAG failure on its own (the toast does name the problem in text), but 3.3.1 and 4.1.3 are close; the schedule dialog shows the product already has the better pattern. A screen-reader announcement of the toast was not tested (no real screen reader), so whether it is announced at all is unknown.

## Evidence

![Save pressed with no entity chosen: dialog unchanged, no visible message](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-005-validation-toast-only.png?raw=1)

Desktop 1280x760, light theme, English, 200 ms after pressing Save. The message "Pick a switch or valve" exists in the DOM (`div.toast.error`, `role=status`) and was gone 3.2 s later; it is not visible in the picture (BUG-012). Fields after Save: `aria-invalid` 0, `required` 0, focus on Save.

## Recommendation

Use the schedule dialog's pattern for zone and plan dialogs: inline `field-error` with `role="alert"`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, focus the first invalid field, mark required fields. Keep toasts for confirmations only.

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

**Fix** (commit `3ae4dc7`, `www/panel.js`): shared `fieldError(id)` / `setFieldError(node, input, msg)` helpers (the schedule dialog's pattern, made generic). Zone dialog: inline errors under Zone name, the entity list and Flow rate (opening "Show advanced" when the flow rate is the problem), alongside the existing Minutes error; all problems shown at once, focus on the first invalid field in page order, `aria-invalid` + `aria-describedby` on fields (the entity list is a group: described only), `aria-required` on Zone name and Minutes. Plan dialog: errors under Plan name and the zone steps, focus on the name (or "Add zone" when the steps are empty). Errors clear as soon as the field is fixed. The v0.14.1 toast still shows the first message as a second signal.
