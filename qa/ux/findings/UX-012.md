## Problem

1. Press **Water now** on a zone card: the status card reads "Started by: service" and Recent activity "Front lawn: started / service" (he: "שירות", de: "Dienst"). The panel calls the `run_valve` service and records source `service`, although a source label "manual" exists. To the gardener "service" is not a thing they did.
2. Recent activity is collapsed by default, at the bottom of Home, under the week view. It is a flat log of one row per event ("cancelled", "started"), 14 groups at most, with no duration, no total, no "watered 3 zones for 30 min". Yesterday's answer is: scroll, expand, count rows, and a busy week pushes yesterday out. Reports is a separate button inside the same card header.
3. "Skipped" rows in the log read the same weight as started rows, so "did it water because of rain or not" needs reading every line.

## Why it matters

"What happened yesterday" is the third task on the brief and the one a gardener checks every morning. The data exists; the screen does not answer the question.

## Evidence

![Status card: Started by: service after pressing Water now](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-012-started-by-service.png?raw=1)

## Recommendation

(a) Label panel and card starts "Manual (Water now)" or "You"; keep "voice", "schedule", "calendar". (b) Add a one-line "Last watering: yesterday 06:00, 3 zones, 30 min" (or "Skipped yesterday: rain") above the zone cards, and open Recent activity by default or group it by day with a total per day. Product wording (model:opus).
