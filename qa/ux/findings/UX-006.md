## Problem

The bar has `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"` and `aria-selected`, which tells a screen reader that the standard tab keyboard pattern applies. It does not: ArrowRight on the first tab leaves focus and selection unchanged, every tab is a Tab stop (no roving tabindex), tabs have no `aria-controls`, and the `tabpanel` has no name. The page has four tabs, not six: Reports is a view under Home and the week view is a card on Home.

## Why it matters

A screen-reader user is told "tab, 1 of 4" and will press arrow keys; nothing happens. The tabs are still reachable and operable with Tab and Enter, so this works; it is worse than it needs to be. Not filed as a WCAG failure. Also, after opening Reports the tab bar still shows Home as selected and there is no heading-level change announced; see BUG-002 for the focus loss on activation.

## Evidence

Evidence unavailable as an image: the behaviour is keyboard-only. Measured: ArrowRight on tab 1 leaves `document.activeElement` on tab 1 and `aria-selected` on Home; all four tabs are Tab stops (Tab x4 visited Home, Zones, Programs, Settings); `tabpanel` unnamed in `ariaSnapshot`. Code: `panel.js` L751-764.

## Recommendation

Implement the ARIA tabs pattern (roving `tabindex`, Left/Right/Home/End, `aria-controls` and `aria-labelledby` on the panel; in RTL Left and Right swap), or drop `role="tab"` and use plain buttons with `aria-current`.

## Kind

Works, could be better.

## Handback (qa-developer, 2026-10-01)

**Fix** (commit `3ae4dc7`, `www/panel.js` `_render`): ARIA tabs pattern with automatic activation. Only the selected tab is a Tab stop (`tabindex` 0 / -1); ArrowRight / ArrowLeft move to and select the next / previous tab, wrapping (mirrored in RTL: ArrowLeft is "next" in Hebrew / Arabic); Home / End go to the first / last. Tabs have ids `sw-tab-<key>` and `aria-controls="sw-tabpanel"`; the tab panel has that id and `aria-labelledby` the selected tab. Focus lands on the newly selected tab after the re-render.
