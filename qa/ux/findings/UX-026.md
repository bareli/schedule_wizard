## Problem

1. Day header "≈ 302 L" has no noun; at 320 px "≈" and "302 L" wrap onto separate lines, and the litres sit next to the temperatures, reading like a weather value. The run dialog says "About 28 L of water".
2. German boost banner: **"Bewässerung +30 % am Sa 10.10.."** (the date already ends with a period).
3. Settings hint: "Check Weather tips under Send notification when to get them as a notification with buttons the evening before." Two label names run together without quotes; hard to parse in all three languages.
4. Tips card: "To get these tips on your phone, pick a notification target in Settings." is plain text, no link.
5. Timeline: the amber "now" line is not in the legend.
6. Frost tip button "Skip early runs" never says what early means (before 10:00); in Hebrew the button says "השקיות בוקר" while the text says "ההשקיות המוקדמות".

![Week list at 320 px, the ≈ sign wraps away from the litres](https://github.com/bareli/schedule_wizard/blob/main/qa/evidence/UX-026-week-en-320.png?raw=1)

Point 2: de 320 Home text dump ("Bewässerung +30 % am Sa 10.10.."). Points 3-6 are visible in the UX-021, UX-022 and UX-024 screenshots.

## Recommendation

1. "≈ 302 L water" / "≈ 302 l Wasser" / "≈ 302 ליטר מים", with a no-break space after ≈.
2. Strip a trailing period from the date before the sentence period (or drop the sentence period in de `boost.planned_day` / `boost.planned`).
3. en: **To get these tips the evening before, tick "Weather tips (evening)" under "Send notification when".** de: **Für Tipps am Vorabend aktiviere „Wettertipps (abends)" unter „Benachrichtigen bei".** he: **כדי לקבל את הטיפים בערב שלפני, יש לסמן „טיפים לפי מזג האוויר (ערב)" תחת „שליחת התראה כאשר".**
4. Make "in Settings" a link that opens Settings with "Send notifications to" focused.
5. Add "Now" to the timeline legend.
6. Frost text **"Consider skipping the runs before 10:00: Herb bed."**, button **"Skip runs before 10:00"**; he "דילוג על השקיות לפני 10:00".
