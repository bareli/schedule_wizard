## Problem

Small points, none blocking:

1. Option text **"Every N days"** (de "Alle N Tage", he "כל N ימים") shows a literal N. Fine for developers, slightly abstract for gardeners; "Every 2 to 30 days" or an "Every other day" quick choice matches how johnpower phrased it.
2. Count label **"Every how many days (2 to 30)"** is clumsy (de "Alle wie viele Tage"). "Repeat every ... days" is the usual form.
3. Entering **1** gives "Enter a whole number from 2 to 30": correct, but a user who wants every day is not pointed to "Days of the week". Add "For every day, use Days of the week."
4. Interval mode has 9 fields; at 320x568 Save sits below the fold. After a failed Save the invalid field is focused and scrolled into view, so errors are findable (good), but the Save button is not sticky.
5. Spacing: status text uses "0 %" in en/de/es/fr but "0%" in he and in the Settings preview strings of every language ("factor 0%"). Pick one.
6. Skip notification text is English-only (see UX-002).

## Why it matters

Each is cheap to fix, and together they remove the last moments of hesitation in the one flow the forum asked for.
