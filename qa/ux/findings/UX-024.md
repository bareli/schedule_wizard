## Problem

Setup: Monday's Herb bed run moved "This time only" to 5:20 AM, so it overlaps Drip line trees 5:00-5:45 (undone afterwards).

Finding the overlap works well: red cards with "same time as Drip line trees" in the list, red bars and a legend in the timeline. Fixing it does not:

1. The **run dialog** says "Waters at the same time as Drip line trees (5:00 AM). Water pressure may drop." and "Move a time, or set Zones at once in Settings." but has **no Move button**; the user must work out a free time and type it. The schedule editor already computes one ("Move to 6:35 AM").
2. "set Zones at once in Settings" is plain text. In Settings > More options there is no topic called Zones at once: it sits inside **"Allow plans at the same time - Off - Normally one plan runs at a time"**, whose title and Off pill describe plans, not zones (and Off there does not mean zones are limited; the default is "No limit").
3. Schedule editor warning while editing Herb bed: **"Waters at the same time as Morning lawn (Sun 6:00 AM), Herb bed (Sun, Oct 18, 6:05 AM)."** The zone is listed as clashing with itself (another Herb bed time), and the two dates use different formats.
4. After "Move to", the warning box disappears with no confirmation that the new time is clear.

## Why it matters

"Find and fix overlapping times" is a core v0.16.0 task. The product shows the problem in red and then sends the user to calculate times by hand or to hunt for a setting under the wrong heading.

## Recommendation

- Run dialog overlap box: add buttons with the computed free time: **"Move to 5:45 AM, this day only"** and **"Move to 5:45 AM, every time"** (opens the editor pre-filled). Sentence: "Waters at the same time as Drip line trees (5:00-5:45 AM). Water pressure may drop."
- Make "Zones at once" in the hints a link that opens Settings > More options with that group expanded and the select focused.
- Rename the group to **"Watering at the same time"**, description "How many zones and plans may water together, for good water pressure.", pill showing the real state, e.g. "Plans: one at a time · Zones: no limit".
- Editor, clash with the same zone: **"Herb bed already waters at 6:05 AM on Sundays (another watering time)."** One date format: "Sun 6:00 AM", and "Sun 10/18, 6:05 AM" only for a single date.
- After Move to: a short line "6:35 AM is free." until the next edit.
